This project filters the Bitfinex BTC/USD archive to January-June 2015 and derives hypothetical trade ledgers, statistical summaries and figures. These are research transformations, not original exchange trade records or endorsement by the data publisher. Raw archives are obtained separately from https://bitcoinpricedata.com/downloads/ .
